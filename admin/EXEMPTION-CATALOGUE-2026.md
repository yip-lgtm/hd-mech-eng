# VTC 2026/27 standing exemption catalogue vs EG524701

Source: 《高級文憑 / DFS / DVE 單元豁免目錄 2026/27》Aug 2026 ed. Staff/student reference.

This list is **pre-approved tick-box routes** (mainly VTC DVE/DFS/DVB, HKDSE, PSC, Gaokao, IGCSE).  
Overseas university modules that are **not printed here** still go through department case-by-case mapping (transcript + syllabus + ILO). They are not refused by this booklet — they are simply not automatic.

## HD Mechanical Engineering rows that exist

| Code | Title | Printed criterion | Your match |
|---|---|---|---|
| EME3210 | Fundamental Mathematics for ME | DVB Math 3; **or HKDSE Math Compulsory L2+**; or DFS Foundation Math III | Auto **only if** you hold HKDSE Math ≥2. US Calc is higher-level but not printed — file as equivalent / case-by-case |
| EME3212 | Drawing and CAD | DVE AI&R CAD; or DVE Watch & Clock solid model + CAD | No. ENGR 1204 is not listed |
| EME3226 | ME Principles | DVB Mechanical Principles **and** Engineering Science | No |
| EME3228 | Basic Electricity & Electronics | DVB Electrical & Electronic Principles **and** Microcontroller System | No |

Not in the booklet at all (so not automatic): EME3211 Math I, EME3227 S&D, EME4215 MoM, EME4202 Fluids, EME4212 Math II, EME2201/2202 Practical, EME4290 IA, ITE3009.

Building Services IA (EME4308) *is* listed (1 year FT + 100 relevant hours) — that wording is useful when asking PC about EME4290, but it is a different code/programme.

## Language (all HD)

| Code | Printed criterion that could apply to you |
|---|---|
| LAN3003 | PSC Putonghua 三級甲; **or** Gaokao Chinese ≥80; or named VTC generic units |
| LAN3107 | HKDSE Eng L4 **and** Speaking paper L4; or IGCSE Eng B after DVB; or named VTC 75+ modules |
| LAN3108 | HKDSE Eng L4 **and** Writing paper L4; or AS Use of English E + Practical Skills |

US ENGL 1301/1302 A and SPCH 1315 A are **not** printed. Do not file language unless you also have DSE/PSC/Gaokao.

## WPD (all HD) — this is the listed door for you

SDD4005 / 4006 / 4007 each allow:

- comparable academic achievements; **or**
- pass the exemption assessment **plus 2-year full-time work experience**

File WPD on that clause in Sem 2–5, not via the engineering table.

## How to use this with CS / PC

1. If you have **HKDSE Math ≥ 2**: tick EME3210 off the catalogue. Fast.
2. File EME3211 as **credit transfer / mapping** (Calc I–III + DE + LA). Quote that it is absent from the booklet so it must be assessed by the department.
3. Do not tick EME3212, LAN3003, LAN3107 from this booklet.
4. Keep attending until the letter.
