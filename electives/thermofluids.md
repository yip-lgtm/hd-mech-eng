## Notes 筆記

- Steam Rankine: Four kit: pump, boiler, turbine, condenser. Label 1–2–3–4
  - 蒸汽 Rankine：四件：泵、鍋爐、汽輪、冷凝。先標 1–2–3–4
- Steam Rankine: Isentropic turbine is ideal; real uses ηt
  - 蒸汽 Rankine：汽輪等熵係理想；真實用等熵效率 ηt
- Steam Rankine: Reheat and regeneration raise mean heat-addition T
  - 蒸汽 Rankine：再熱同回熱為咗提高平均吸熱溫度
- Gas Brayton: Compressor, combustor, turbine. Open cycle treats air as the fluid
  - 燃氣 Brayton：壓氣機、燃燒室、渦輪。開式用空氣當工質
- Gas Brayton: Too high a pressure ratio and compressor work eats the turbine
  - 燃氣 Brayton：壓比太高，壓氣機功食晒渦輪功
- Gas Brayton: TIT is limited by blade material
  - 燃氣 Brayton：TIT 受葉片材料限制
- Refrigeration and exchangers: Evaporate, compress, condense, expansion valve
  - 製冷同熱交換器：蒸發、壓縮、冷凝、膨脹閥
- Refrigeration and exchangers: COP over 1 is not a perpetual-motion machine — it moves heat
  - 製冷同熱交換器：COP 大於 1 唔代表永動機，只係搬熱
- Refrigeration and exchangers: LMTD: counterflow beats parallel
  - 製冷同熱交換器：LMTD：逆流平均溫差大過順流

## Workshop application 工作室

  - 把熱力同流體用落蒸汽循環、燃氣循環、製冷同熱交換器。輪機同噴射引擎都要呢科嘅語言。

## Formulas 公式

- η_Carnot = 1 − Tc/Th — 卡諾上限
- η_Rankine = (h1−h2−|wp|) / (h1−h4) — 淨功／吸熱
- COP_R = Q_c / W_net — 製冷性能係數
- Q = U A ΔT_lm — 熱交換器
