## Notes 筆記

- Geometry: 1435 mm is standard gauge; narrow gauge is another family
  - 幾何：軌距 1435 mm 係標準；窄軌另一套
- Geometry: Cant balances centripetal demand; cant deficiency is leftover accel
  - 幾何：超高平衡離心力；不足超高有未平衡加速度
- Geometry: Transition blends tangent cant into the circular curve
  - 幾何：緩和曲線把直線超高同圓曲線接順
- Structure: Ballast can be tamped; slab needs less work, different vibration path
  - 結構：道碴可搗固；板式少養護、振動路徑唔同
- Structure: Fastening stiffness is part of the primary ride
  - 結構：扣件彈性決定一系剛度的一部分
- Structure: Wear and corrugation need grinding
  - 結構：鋼軌磨耗同波浪要打磨
- Turnouts: Switch blades steer; the frog is the crossing
  - 道岔：尖軌搬進路；轍叉係交叉心
- Turnouts: Check rail stops the opposite wheel dropping into the gap
  - 道岔：護軌防止對側輪跌入有害空間
- Turnouts: Higher turnout number → higher diverging speed
  - 道岔：道岔號數愈大，側向通過速度愈高
