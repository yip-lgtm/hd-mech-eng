## Notes 筆記

- Tube structure: Underframe takes long loads; sides and roof close the tube
  - 筒體結構：底架承縱向，側牆同車頂合成筒
- Tube structure: Door/window cuts need pad-up or stress concentrates
  - 筒體結構：開口（門窗）要補強，否則應力集中
- Tube structure: Against MoM: beam + thin-shell entry
  - 筒體結構：對照材料力學：梁 + 薄殼入口
- Materials and fire: Al is light, weld procedure matters; stainless resists corrosion
  - 材料同防火：鋁輕、焊要工序；不銹鋼耐蝕
- Materials and fire: Interior materials: low fire, smoke, toxicity
  - 材料同防火：內裝材料要難燃、低煙、低毒
- Materials and fire: Cable penetrations need fire stopping
  - 材料同防火：電纜貫穿要封阻
- Ends and interior: Crush zone is not the cab survival space
  - 車端同內裝：吸能區不可做司機生存空間
- Ends and interior: Coupler height and stroke must match the other car
  - 車端同內裝：車鉤高度同行程要同對向車相容
- Ends and interior: Next RST II: maintenance, bogie detail, systems
  - 車端同內裝：下一步 RST II：維修、轉向架細、系統
