## Notes 筆記

- Train and energy: Barrel stores energy; the train reduces speed and multiplies torque
  - 輪系同能量：發條盒儲能，輪系減速兼放大力矩
- Train and energy: Centre, third, fourth, escape
  - 輪系同能量：中心輪、三番、四番、擒縱輪
- Train and energy: The seconds hand usually sits on the fourth wheel
  - 輪系同能量：秒針通常在四番輪
- Escapement and balance: The escapement chops rotation into ticks
  - 擒縱同擺輪：擒縱把連續轉動拆成一格一格
- Escapement and balance: Hairspring sets frequency; regulator pins change effective length
  - 擒縱同擺輪：擺輪游絲決定頻率；快慢針改有效長度
- Escapement and balance: 28800 vph = 4 Hz = 8 beats/s
  - 擒縱同擺輪：28800 vph = 4 Hz = 8 拍／秒
- Assembly habits: Orientation, oil quantity, dust; too little oil beats a flood
  - 裝配習慣：方向、油量、防塵；少油好過油浸
- Assembly habits: Endshake and backlash: too tight stops, too loose rattles
  - 裝配習慣：端隙同齒側隙太緊會停，太鬆會響
- Assembly habits: Against SolidWorks: fine-module gears and pivot-hole fits
  - 裝配習慣：對照 SolidWorks：細模數齒輪同軸孔公差

## Workshop application 工作室

- Mechanical watches: gear train, escapement, barrel, balance. Miniature precision mechanics — useful for design and the workshop.
  - 機械錶：齒輪系、擒縱、發條盒、擺輪。精密機械同公差嘅縮小版，對設計同工場都有用。

## Formulas 公式

- T = 2π √(I / κ) — 擺輪週期
- i = z2 / z1 — 齒數比
- f = 1 / T    (如 4 Hz = 28800 vph) — 振動頻率
