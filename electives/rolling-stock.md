## Notes 筆記

- Bogies: Wheelset, axlebox, frame, air spring
  - 轉向架：輪對、軸箱、構架、空氣彈簧
- Bogies: Primary for high-frequency wheel, secondary for carbody comfort
  - 轉向架：一系管輪軌高頻，二系管車體舒適
- Bogies: Flange and tread: curves by creep, not just the flange
  - 轉向架：輪緣同踏面：通過曲線靠蠕滑
- Traction and brake: Adhesion: rain drops μ, wheelslip protection
  - 牽引同制動：黏着：雨天 μ 跌，空轉保護
- Traction and brake: Electric brake first; air covers low speed and holding
  - 牽引同制動：電制動優先，空氣制動補低速同停妥
- Traction and brake: Regen dumps kinetic energy back to the catenary
  - 牽引同制動：再生把動能交返接觸網
- Carbody interfaces: Coupler loads, gangway seal, door obstacle detection
  - 車體介面：車鉤傳力、貫通道密封、車門障礙檢測
- Carbody interfaces: Fire, evacuation, crash energy management
  - 車體介面：防火、疏散、車頭碰撞能量管理
- Carbody interfaces: Next: track system and RST I
  - 車體介面：下一步讀軌道系統同 RST I

## Workshop application 工作室

- Carbody, bogie, wheel–rail, traction and brake. Gateway to the three railway electives. RST I/II go deeper on body and maintenance.
  - 車體、轉向架、輪軌接觸、牽引同制動。鐵路三科入門。之後 RST I／II 再入維修同車體細節。
