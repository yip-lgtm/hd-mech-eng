## Notes 筆記

- Part rhythm: One feature, one intent; do not draw the whole part in one sketch
  - 零件節奏：一個特徵一個意圖；唔好一個 sketch 畫成件
- Part rhythm: Boss the bulk, Cut the removal
  - 零件節奏：Boss 先大件，Cut 再減料
- Part rhythm: Part 2: 80×50×15 then cut 33×25
  - 零件節奏：件 2：80×50×15 再剪 33×25
- Assembly: Fix the first part; then coincident, concentric, distance
  - 組合：第一件固定；之後重合、同心、距離
- Assembly: Interference check before drawing
  - 組合：干涉檢查先於出圖
- Assembly: Exploded view tells the build order
  - 組合：爆炸圖講裝配順序
- Drawings: 1st/3rd angle by standard; HD shops often use 1st
  - 工程圖：第一角／第三角跟標準；HD 工場常用第一角
- Drawings: No duplicate dims; start from datums
  - 工程圖：尺寸唔重複、由基準出發
- Drawings: BOM matches part numbers
  - 工程圖：BOM 同零件號一致
