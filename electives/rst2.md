## Notes 筆記

- Maintenance regime: Preventive by km/calendar; condition-based by monitors
  - 維修制度：預防性：按公里／日曆；狀態修：按監測
- Maintenance regime: Bogie overhaul strips bogies; general overhaul strips systems
  - 維修制度：架修拆轉向架，大修拆車體系統
- Maintenance regime: Records and part numbers; do not mix cars
  - 維修制度：紀錄同零件號，唔好混車
- Wheelsets and brake: Turn the tread; diameter mismatch has a limit
  - 輪對同制動：鏇輪恢復踏面；直徑差有限度
- Wheelsets and brake: Bearing temperature is an alarm, not a wait-and-seize
  - 輪對同制動：軸承溫升係警報，唔係等佢咬死
- Wheelsets and brake: Brake test: hold, leak, stopping distance
  - 輪對同制動：制動試驗：保壓、泄漏、停車距離
- System interfaces: TCMS bus for status; fault codes before stripping parts
  - 系統介面：TCMS 匯流排睇狀態；故障碼先於拆件
- System interfaces: ATP brakes overspeed; ATO is driving automation
  - 系統介面：ATP 超速要制動；ATO 係駕駛自動化
- System interfaces: With the automation elective: a train is a closed loop too
  - 系統介面：同自動化選修：列車都係閉環控制

## Workshop application 工作室

- Planned maintenance, bogie overhaul, brake test, train-control interface. I is structure, II is operation.
  - 計劃維修、轉向架分解、制動試驗、列車控制介面。I 科構造、II 科運用。

## Formulas 公式

- wear ∝ ∫ p v dt — 磨耗入口
