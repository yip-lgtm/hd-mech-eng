## Notes 筆記

- Gas path: Intake ram → compressor → burn → turbine → nozzle
  - 氣體路徑：進氣減速增壓 → 壓氣機 → 燃燒 → 渦輪 → 噴管
- Gas path: Turbine work feeds the compressor first; leftover becomes jet
  - 氣體路徑：渦輪功先餵壓氣機，剩低變動能
- Gas path: Turbofan: lots of slower bypass air, better efficiency
  - 氣體路徑：渦扇：外涵道走大量慢空氣，效率高
- Performance numbers: Pressure ratio and TIT cap thermal efficiency
  - 性能數字：壓比同 TIT 決定熱效率上限
- Performance numbers: Lower SFC is thriftier; civil engines want high BPR
  - 性能數字：SFC 愈細愈慳油；高旁通民用機
- Performance numbers: Map onto the Applied Thermo Fluids Brayton lesson
  - 性能數字：對照應用熱流體嘅 Brayton 課
- Maintenance entry: FOD: intake inspection, nicked blades
  - 維修入口：FOD：進氣口檢查、葉片缺口
- Maintenance entry: Hot section: combustor and blade oxidation/cracks
  - 維修入口：熱端：燃燒室、渦輪葉片氧化同裂
- Maintenance entry: This is not an aircraft-maintenance licence; it is the physics
  - 維修入口：唔好當飛機維修牌；呢科係原理

## Workshop application 工作室

## Formulas 公式

- F = ṁe ve − ṁ0 v0 + (pe−p0)Ae — 淨推力
- SFC = ṁf / F — 推力燃油消耗
- BPR = ṁ_bypass / ṁ_core — 旁通比
