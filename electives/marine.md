## Notes 筆記

- Main engine and cycles: Slow 2-stroke crosshead: long stroke, direct propeller
  - 主機同循環：慢速二衝程十字頭：長衝程、直連螺旋槳
- Main engine and cycles: Medium 4-stroke: gearbox, gensets, aux propulsion
  - 主機同循環：中速四衝程：齒輪箱、發電機、輔推進
- Main engine and cycles: Sketch the gas path: intake → compress → burn → expand → exhaust
  - 主機同循環：先畫氣路：進氣→壓縮→燃燒→膨脹→排氣
- Fuel, lube, cooling: HFO needs heat, purify, viscosity control before injection
  - 燃油、潤滑、冷卻：重油要加熱、淨化、黏度控制先噴得出
- Fuel, lube, cooling: Cylinder oil ≠ system oil; do not mix duties
  - 燃油、潤滑、冷卻：潤滑分氣缸油同系統油，唔好混用途
- Fuel, lube, cooling: HT/LT cooling circuits; corrosion and scale
  - 燃油、潤滑、冷卻：冷卻水有高溫／低溫回路，防腐同防結垢
- Watch and regs entry: Handover: rpm, T, p, levels, alarms
  - 值班同法規入口：交班：轉速、溫度、壓力、油位、警報
- Watch and regs entry: MARPOL: oil, sewage, garbage, air; log books
  - 值班同法規入口：MARPOL：油、污水、垃圾、大氣；記簿
- Watch and regs entry: This plus Applied Thermo Fluids is the Mardep path
  - 值班同法規入口：呢科加應用熱流體，先講海事處認可

## Workshop application 工作室

- Main diesel, auxiliaries, boilers, pumps, fuel and ballast. Pair with Applied Thermo Fluids for the Marine Department seagoing path. Safety first, then systems.
  - 柴油主機、輔機、鍋爐、泵、燃油同壓載。同應用熱流體一齊選，先對準海事處認可。船用先安全、再系統。

## Formulas 公式

- BMEP · Vd · N / k = Power — 制動平均有效壓力
- SFOC = mf / P  (g/kWh) — 燃油消耗率
- η_prop = P_thrust / P_shaft — 推進效率
